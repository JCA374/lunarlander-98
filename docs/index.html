98% landings in wind, turbulence and past a boulder

A small neural network flies Gymnasium's Lunar Lander in strong gusty wind (power 15), turbulence (1.5) and with a boulder floating on the way down. Goal: ≥ 98% landed between the flags on 1,000 unseen flights, confirmed on a second fresh 1,000. The previous project (NEAT) stalled at about 85%. This time the method is chosen by a measured bake-off.
